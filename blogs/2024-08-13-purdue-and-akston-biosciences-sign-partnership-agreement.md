BEVERLY, Mass. and WEST LAFAYETTE, Ind. – August 13, 2024 – Akston Biosciences Corporation, dedicated to accelerating the biologics revolution in Animal Health, and Purdue University, today announced a strategic partnership to co-develop an anti-cPD-L1 monoclonal antibody (mAb) immunotherapy to treat cancer in dogs.
